# Notifications

## Purpose

Keep users informed about important platform activities.

---

## Objectives

- Deliver timely notifications.
- Improve communication.
- Increase user engagement.

---

## Actors

- Patient
- Clinician
- Administrator

---

## User Stories

As a user, I want to:

- Receive important updates.
- View notification history.
- Mark notifications as read.

---

## Workflow

Business Event

↓

Notification Generated

↓

Notification Delivered

↓

Notification Viewed

---

## Business Rules

- Notifications are generated automatically.
- Users receive only relevant notifications.
- Notifications remain available until deleted by the user.
