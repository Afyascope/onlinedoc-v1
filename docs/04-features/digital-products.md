# Digital Products

## Purpose

Provide downloadable healthcare resources for patients.

---

## Objectives

- Sell digital healthcare resources.
- Deliver downloadable content.
- Improve patient education.

---

## Actors

- Patient
- Administrator

---

## User Stories

### Patient

As a patient, I want to:

- Browse products.
- Purchase products.
- Download purchased products.

### Administrator

As an administrator, I want to:

- Create products.
- Edit products.
- Manage pricing.
- Publish products.

---

## Workflow

Browse Products

↓

View Product

↓

Purchase

↓

Payment

↓

Download

---

## Business Rules

- Products require payment before download.
- Products belong to categories.
- Products may be unpublished.
