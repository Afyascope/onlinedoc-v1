# OnlineDoc Vision

## Vision

To become Kenya's most trusted digital healthcare platform by connecting patients with licensed healthcare professionals through secure, affordable, and accessible online healthcare services.

## Mission

To simplify access to quality healthcare by providing digital consultations, trusted health education, and patient-centered healthcare services through modern technology.

## Problem Statement

Millions of patients experience barriers when accessing healthcare.

Common challenges include:

- Long waiting times
- Travel costs
- Limited access to healthcare professionals
- Delayed medical advice
- Limited access to reliable health education
- Fragmented healthcare services

OnlineDoc aims to reduce these barriers by delivering healthcare digitally while maintaining professional clinical standards.

## Initial Product Focus

Phase 1 focuses on delivering a simple but complete healthcare experience.

Patients should be able to:

- Register an account
- Start a consultation
- Chat with a clinician
- Pay consultation fees
- Purchase digital health booklets
- Access previous consultations

## Long-Term Vision

OnlineDoc will evolve into a comprehensive digital healthcare ecosystem including:

- Audio consultations
- Video consultations
- Online pharmacy
- Laboratory integration
- Electronic Medical Records
- AI-assisted healthcare
- Chronic disease management
- Remote patient monitoring
- Insurance integration
- Hospital partnerships

- Patient First
