# Digital Product Contract

## Purpose

Defines how patients discover and purchase digital healthcare resources.

---

## Actor

Patient

---

## Objective

Allow patients to browse, purchase, and access digital health products.

---

## Input

The frontend provides:

- Product Identifier

---

## Output

The backend returns:

- Product Information
- Price
- Availability
- Purchase Status
- Download Access (after successful payment)

---

## Business Rules

- Only published products are available for purchase.
- Products remain accessible after purchase.
- Products are organized by category.
- Prices are managed by the platform.
