# Project Glossary

## Purpose

This document defines the terminology used throughout the OnlineDoc project.

These definitions provide a common language for documentation, development, and communication.

---

## Administrator

A user responsible for managing the platform, content, users, and system configuration.

---

## Category

A grouping used to organize digital products.

Examples include Nutrition, Diabetes, Pregnancy, and Exercise.

---

## Chat Consultation

A consultation conducted through text-based messaging between a patient and a clinician.

---

## Clinician

A licensed healthcare professional who provides medical consultations through the platform.

---

## Consultation

A healthcare interaction between a patient and a clinician.

A consultation is the primary business entity within OnlineDoc.

---

## Conversation

The communication channel associated with a consultation.

A conversation contains one or more messages.

---

## Dashboard

The main interface presented to authenticated users after login.

Different dashboards exist for patients, clinicians, and administrators.

---

## Digital Product

A downloadable healthcare resource available for purchase.

Examples include meal plans, nutrition guides, and patient education booklets.

---

## Follow-up Consultation

A consultation that continues the management of a previous consultation.

---

## Message

A single communication exchanged during a consultation.

Messages may include text, images, or files.

---

## Notification

A message generated by the system to inform users about important events.

Examples include payment confirmations and new consultation messages.

---

## Order

A record representing a purchase made by a patient.

Orders may include consultations or digital products.

---

## Patient

A registered user who receives healthcare services through OnlineDoc.

---

## Payment

A financial transaction associated with an order.

---

## Platform

The complete OnlineDoc system, including the website, backend, and supporting services.

---

## Profile

The personal and professional information associated with a user account.

---

## Service

A healthcare offering available through the platform.

Examples include chat consultations and urgent consultations.

---

## Urgent Consultation

A consultation that requires higher priority and faster clinician response.

---

## User

Any authenticated person using the platform.

User roles include Patient, Clinician, and Administrator.
