# Project Scope

## In Scope (Phase 1)

### Public Website

- Landing page
- Services
- Pricing
- About
- Contact
- Frequently Asked Questions

### Authentication

- Patient registration
- Patient login
- Password reset
- Email verification

### Patient Dashboard

- Profile
- Consultations
- Chat
- Purchased Booklets
- Orders
- Settings

### Clinician Dashboard

- Dashboard
- Consultation Queue
- Active Consultations
- Patient Information
- Profile

### Consultation Module

- Chat Consultation
- Follow-up Consultation
- Urgent Consultation

### Payments

- Consultation Payments
- Digital Product Payments

### Digital Products

- Meal Plans
- Nutrition Guides
- Patient Education Booklets

### CMS

- Content Management
- Blog
- FAQs
- Health Articles
- Booklets

---

## Out of Scope

The following features are intentionally excluded from Phase 1.

- Video consultations
- Audio consultations
- Online pharmacy
- Laboratory management
- AI diagnosis
- Insurance claims
- Hospital management
- Electronic Medical Records
- Appointment scheduling
- Wearable device integration

These features will be introduced in future phases.
